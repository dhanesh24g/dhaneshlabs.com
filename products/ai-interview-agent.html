Move from generic interview prep to role-specific practice.

Most mock interview tools start with generic question lists. AI Interview Agent starts with the job posting, extracts the company and role context, researches likely interview patterns, ranks the strongest questions, and evaluates the candidate's answers in a structured session.

Workflow

How the product moves from job posting to feedback

01 • Extract

Turn the posting into a job target

The system captures the role context first, with timeout handling and fallback metadata when scraping is unavailable.

02 • Research

Collect interview intelligence

Targeted queries look for company, role, and interview-source signals, then continue with partial data if individual sources fail.

03 • Practice

Run the interview session

The backend owns turn management and transcript state while the frontend presents the voice-first candidate experience.

04 • Improve

Generate the feedback report

The final report turns session answers into scores, improvement areas, strengths, and targeted prep guidance.
