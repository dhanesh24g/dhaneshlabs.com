How the product moves from setup to settlement

01 • Setup

Create the league structure

03 • Match Day

Log results and edge cases

Handle normal results, ties, overrides, and washouts in one place so the settlement path stays consistent.

04 • Ledger

Read the financial picture clearly

End every match cycle with a transparent view of who spent what, who won what, and how balances changed.
