Publishing Pattern

The system to use as you add more products

This keeps the site premium and prevents the Products page from turning into a maintenance problem.

01 • Add a card

Create the product entry on the hub

Add the name, status label, short description, and intended category without disturbing the rest of the page structure.

02 • Add a page

Give serious products their own detail page

Once a product deserves a real story, create a dedicated URL under `/products/...` and move the deeper content there.

03 • Update labels

Move products between Live and Coming Soon

Status is lightweight, visible, and easy to maintain. It communicates momentum without requiring the full roadmap everywhere.

04 • Offer launch paths

Offer both paths without making the hub feel noisy

Users can open the dedicated page when they want context, or jump into the live app directly from the hub when they already know what they need.
